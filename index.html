Everyone starts somewhere

You Do Not Need to Be Perfect to Begin

You do not need perfect grammar or perfect pronunciation. You only need the willingness to begin.

At Talk English, we believe confidence grows one conversation at a time — in a friendly, respectful, pressure-free environment.

Practice outside class is optional. You do not have to complete homework, record yourself or submit work. We practise the essentials together in class, with support for your current English and goals.

Meet the founder

Hello, I’m George

I created Talk English because I believe learning English is about much more than grammar and vocabulary. It is about building confidence, connecting with people, and opening new opportunities.

I have met many talented adults who understood English but were afraid to speak. That is why I created a place where learners can ask questions, make mistakes, practise real situations, and improve one conversation at a time.

In 2005–2006, I completed 93 volunteer hours in the Adult Education Program of the Toronto Catholic District School Board in Canada.

“You do not have to be perfect. You only have to be willing to begin — and I will support you along the way.”

Why I teach

English Can Change the Opportunities in Your Life

When I moved to Canada and rebuilt my life, I learned how important communication is for work, relationships, and feeling that you belong.

Those experiences taught me that adult learners do not need more pressure. They need practical guidance, patience, and a teacher who believes they can improve.

Whether your goal is to communicate at work, prepare for an interview, travel, or simply speak with greater confidence, I will be happy to support you on that journey.

We believe every conversation is an opportunity to grow, and every student deserves a teacher who believes in them.

Frequently asked questions


Have Questions?


Clear answers before you book a trial lesson.







I am a beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Beginner English is designed for new learners and adults who have studied before but still lack speaking confidence.





How are lessons delivered?

Lessons are taught live through Microsoft Teams. You may join from a computer, tablet, or phone.





Is Thai support available?

Written Thai guidance is available on the website. Personal enquiries are answered by Teacher George.





Is this an IELTS preparation course?

No. Talk English focuses on practical communication, workplace English, and speaking confidence.





What do I need for an online lesson?

You need an internet-connected device, headphones or speakers, a microphone, and a reasonably quiet place.




How will my registration information be used?
Your information is processed only to deliver the registration to Ploy and George and to contact you about the trial lesson, scheduling, and programs. Talk English does not sell personal information.


What if I need to reschedule?
Contact Ploy or George as early as possible. Replacement times depend on availability and will be confirmed in writing.


How do payment and refunds work?
The trial lesson is free. Tuition, payment methods, rescheduling, and refund conditions for paid courses will be confirmed in writing before you pay.
